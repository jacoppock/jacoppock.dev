## The problem

Clinicians drown in data and have little time. Patients want care that fits them.

## My role

I led Data Science & AI. Bionic Health was acquired in 2025 and is now Thrive Wellness.

## Approach

- **Personalized risk profiles.** An automated system that ingests multi-modal patient data (imaging, EHR, blood biomarkers, continuous glucose, blood pressure, sleep, heart rate) and produces disease-risk profiles, including a 30-year cardiovascular risk estimate.
- **Clinical decision support.** A FHIR-compliant, agent-based generative-AI framework that distills patient data into evidence-based recommendations for clinicians in real time.
- **Patient engagement.** A retrieval-augmented assistant for onboarding and clinician-approved lifestyle, nutrition and exercise guidance.
