## The problem

Put an inertial sensor inside a football and you get a firehose of acceleration and rotation. Turning that into spin rate, velocity and throw quality in real time is a signal-processing problem.

## My role

Algorithm engineer.

## Approach & outcome

- Real-time tracking and statistics from embedded IMUs.
- Partnered with NFL teams on new ball-tracking technology for player-performance analysis.
