## The problem

Put an inertial sensor inside a football and you get a firehose of acceleration and rotation. Turning that into spin rate, velocity and throw quality in real time is a signal-processing problem.

## My role

Algorithm engineer.

## Approach & outcome

- Algorithms and data pipelines for the Wilson X line of connected footballs and basketballs.
- Field studies with NFL and NCAA teams on whether ball-level data could give athletes and coaches next-generation analytics.
